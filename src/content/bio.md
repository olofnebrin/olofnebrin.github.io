I'm a PhD candidate in Astrophysics. In short, I am interested in the origin of the Universe and all the stuff within it (e.g. galaxies), and understanding the physics that made it possible. 

In more verbose language, my interests span galaxy/star formation and cosmology, with a particular focus on Cosmic Dawn, the epoch when the first stars and galaxies formed. I study this epoch using a combination of analytical methods (pen-and-paper calculations) and computer simulations. 

When I'm not thinking about the Universe and science, my hobbies include powerlifting/bodybuilding, music/concerts, and drawing (occasionally).
